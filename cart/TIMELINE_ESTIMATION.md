## TOTAL PROJECT TIMELINE

### Fast Track (Aggressive)
- **Duration**: **16-20 weeks** (~4-5 months)
- **Team Size**: 6-8 people
- **Daily Standups**: Required
- **Scope Compression**: Minimum viable features only
- **Risk**: Higher bug rate, quality concerns

- **Duration**: **20-24 weeks** (~5-6 months)
- **Team Size**: 6-8 people
- **Daily Standups**: Yes
- **Scope**: Complete feature set
- **Risk**: Manageable, predictable

- **Duration**: **24-28 weeks** (~6-7 months)
- **Team Size**: 4-5 people
- **Meetings**: 3x/week
- **Scope**: Complete + extra features
- **Risk**: Low, high quality output
